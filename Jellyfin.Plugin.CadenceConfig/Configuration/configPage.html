Deezer playlist sync

Importing a Deezer playlist (via the Cadence client) creates a matching Jellyfin playlist and subscribes it. The "Sync Deezer Playlists" scheduled task then keeps each playlist up to date, adding tracks as they arrive in your library (e.g. after Lidarr fills in a previously-missing artist). It only adds — it never removes tracks.

How often the sync task runs. Default 12. Takes effect after the next Jellyfin restart.

Storyteller (Whispersync + read-along)

With a Storyteller URL set, each user connects their own Storyteller account from the Cadence client's Settings. Listening positions are pushed to Storyteller as they are saved, Storyteller reading positions are pulled back by the "Sync Storyteller positions" task, and Cadence can show the aligned text karaoke-style while a book plays. Tokens stay on this server.

Storyteller base URL (e.g. http://192.168.7.211:8001). Leave blank to turn the feature off.
How often Storyteller reading positions are pulled into Jellyfin. Default 5. Takes effect after the next Jellyfin restart.
